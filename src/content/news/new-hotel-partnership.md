Thivi Edu has signed a partnership with four hotels in the region to offer paid internships to students of the Hospitality program. The agreement was built together with the hotels, based on the skills they most look for when hiring, such as guest service, event setup and teamwork under pressure.

Starting next semester, students will spend part of their course working in restaurants, banquet halls and reception areas, always supervised by a professional from the hotel and by a teacher from our school. The internships last three months and include a monthly payment, so students can gain experience without leaving their studies behind.

The school expects that many interns will receive job offers before graduating, as already happens with our other partners. Selection is based on academic performance and attendance, and interviews will be held at the hotels. Details about the process will be published in the student portal in the coming weeks.
