The new Thivi Edu student portal is now available to all enrolled students. It replaces the old mix of emails, printed schedules and messaging groups with a single place where everything about the course can be found, from the first day of class to the final evaluation.

Through the portal, students can check their weekly schedules, download recipes and class materials, follow their grades and send messages directly to instructors. Notifications warn about changes in the timetable, so no one arrives at the kitchen on the wrong day or at the wrong time.

The portal works on computers, tablets and phones, and was tested with a group of students before the launch. Their feedback helped us simplify the menus and make the most used features easier to reach. Access data was sent by email to every student, and the secretariat is available to help with the first login.
