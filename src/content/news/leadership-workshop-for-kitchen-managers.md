Thivi Edu's leadership workshop focuses on the practical challenges faced by kitchen managers and hospitality team leaders. It explores ways to organize daily work, communicate clearly, and make decisions during busy service.

Short discussions are paired with practical exercises in constructive feedback, conflict resolution, shift planning, and budget basics. The scenarios draw on common hospitality situations, helping participants connect leadership ideas with day-to-day operations.

The workshop is intended for current students and professionals in hospitality. Contact the Thivi Edu team for information about future sessions.
